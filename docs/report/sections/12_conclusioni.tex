\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This project delivers a reproducible four-model, from-scratch DDPM family for unconditional and structured text-conditioned CartoonSet avatar generation. The conditional evidence supports a carefully limited account of the five-endpoint chain: CA16 is associated with higher measured adherence at 20k under independent trajectories, and the H7 and V2-A training extensions are authenticated exact-state continuations. These statements are not single-factor causal estimates.

The final release keeps conditional structured-prompt evaluation separate from unconditional marginal sampling. Common-32 quality makes an explicitly derived common-space measurement available, but it does not rank native detail; conditional and unconditional LPIPS measure different diversity estimands. The dual-view evaluator is an independent instrument with a documented synthetic native64 background-sensitivity limitation. Together with provenance-identified checkpoints, saved summaries, CPU-by-default notebooks, and GPU-validated UI inference, these disclosures make the released evidence traceable without overstating what its metrics establish.
