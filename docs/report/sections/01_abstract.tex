\section*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Abstract}
We study compact pixel-space denoising diffusion probabilistic models (DDPMs) for CartoonSet avatar generation, trained from scratch with a structured four-attribute caption interface. The final release contains exactly four public EMA endpoints in a $2\times2$ design: V1 unconditional and H7 conditional at native $32\times32$ after 40k successful updates, and V2-A unconditional and V2-A conditional at native $64\times64$ after 60k updates. At each resolution the conditional endpoint shares the image backbone and adds a scratch-trained text encoder with cross-attention at $8\times8$ and $16\times16$. Generator training uses CartoonSet10k; the later conditional measurement instrument is an independently trained CartoonSet100k dual-view evaluator.

The report separates two estimands. On the authenticated supplementary structured-prompt panel, H7-40k obtains Common-32 KID/FID of .010000/59.430 and OOD MAA/EAM/MacroPSR of .965/.860/.895; V2-A conditional-60k obtains .016694/65.007 and .980/.920/.960, respectively. For unconditional marginal sampling, STEP~13 reports V1-40k KID/FID/marginal LPIPS of .008134/41.547/.088892 and V2-A-60k of .009724/47.215/.234633. FID is exploratory. Structured adherence is not defined for unconditional samples, and conditional within-prompt LPIPS is not the same quantity as unconditional marginal LPIPS. Common-32 quality downscales native64 images and does not establish native-detail superiority. We retain the five-endpoint conditional ablation chain and its continuation evidence, while explicitly disclosing trajectory, capacity, resolution, preprocessing, and evaluator-domain confounders.

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} DDPM, text-conditioned generation, cross-attention, CartoonSet, compositional generalization, evaluation reliability.
